NGF COLLEGE PROJECT 04: SOLAR ENERGY SELF-CONSUMPTION ANALYTICS
 All observations are synthetic teaching data, not real organization records.
 GRAIN: one building meter per day. Key: date + entity_id.
 Period: July 1-August 29, 2026 inclusive; 12 entities x 60 dates = 720 expected.
 Entity IDs E01-E12: E01-E04 North; E05-E08 Central; E09-E12 South.
 segment is a fixed reporting label, not an additional grain dimension.
 date: ISO YYYY-MM-DD normally. Numeric source columns: generation_kwh, load_kwh, export_kwh, import_kwh.
 Units follow column suffixes: INR money, kwh energy, kl kilolitres, kg kilograms,
 km distance, litres fuel, seconds/minutes time. Other columns are item/event counts.
 Counts must be whole numbers; fuel_litres may have two decimals. All source
 measures must be finite and >=0. The project context below defines denominators.
 Daily netting hides within-day imports and exports. These simplified meters cannot support tariff savings or battery sizing.
 Logical validation relationships: export_kwh <= generation_kwh; import_kwh <= load_kwh.
 These relationships are business flags to report, not permission to discard
 structurally valid observations. Reject invalid types, unknown IDs/segments,
 negative numbers and fractional counts; retain plausible business discrepancies.

 CLEANING POLICY - ALL PROJECTS USE THE SAME AUDIT CONVENTION
 Raw file: 726 rows. Preserve source_row = physical CSV line (header is line 1).
 Normalize 3 entity IDs with whitespace/lowercase. Parse 2 DD/MM/YYYY dates using
 explicit formats. Recover 2 blank segments from the fixed entity mapping.
 Two generation_kwh fields are formatted with two decimal places and optional comma
 separators. Parse numeric strings and record normalization when formatting
 differs from a canonical value. This is a parsing case, not a new measurement.
 Three exact duplicate records: after normalization, keep first and audit removals.
 One impossible date and one out-of-period row containing 'unknown': quarantine.
 E06 on July 1 has two conflicting records: quarantine BOTH after exact dedup.
 Do not average or choose one conflicting value. Expected clean rows: 719.
 Audit columns: source_row, action, reason. Actions: repaired, dropped_duplicate,
 quarantined. Log repaired fields individually; combine rejection reasons per row.
 Expected removals: 3 duplicate rows and 4 quarantined physical rows.
 Never mutate the raw CSV. Do not hard-code row positions when finding problems.
 The two conflicting records are not two legitimate observations.

 BUSINESS ANOMALIES TO RETAIN
 E01 has a low-generation day on July 21.
 E07 on August 10 is a documented full closure/measurement exclusion: all activity and denominator fields are zero; retain the record and display undefined rates as NaN.
 Other day-to-day variation is normal simulated activity, not a deletion rule.

 STUDENT A CONTRACT
 load_and_clean(path) -> (clean, audit), both pandas DataFrames.
 clean keeps all original columns, date is datetime64, numeric columns numeric,
 sorted by date then entity_id with a reset index. The audit retains source lines.

 STUDENT B CONTRACT
 calculate_metrics(clean) -> (detail, daily, entities). Work on a copy.
 detail retains clean columns and adds:
 self_consumed_kwh = generation_kwh-export_kwh
balance_gap_kwh = generation_kwh+import_kwh-load_kwh-export_kwh
 rate_numerator = generation_kwh-export_kwh
 rate_denominator = generation_kwh
 self_consumption_pct = 100 * rate_numerator / rate_denominator.
 safe(a,b) in the definitions means a/b if b!=0, otherwise NaN; you implement it.
 No cross-day balance continuity is assumed for this project.
 daily: date, sums of numeric source columns, rate_numerator, rate_denominator,
 observed_rows, weighted_self_consumption_pct. entities: entity_id, sums of numeric source
 columns, rate_numerator, rate_denominator, weighted_self_consumption_pct.
 Weighted rate = 100 * summed numerator / summed denominator, NaN if zero.
 Sort daily by date and entities by ID. Do not average row percentages.
 Keep full precision; round only displayed values. Percentage columns use points,
 e.g. 75 means 75 percent. Ratios without _pct are multiples or named unit rates.
 Some source sums are calculation controls, not meaningful headline KPIs:
 never present sums of opening/closing snapshots as current balances. For these
 use the last observed date and state it. Summed daily rate denominators may
 repeat carried balances; describe the result as a weighted DAILY rate.
